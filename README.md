# RESTART

RESTART is a web-based digital platform designed to support former inmates in their reintegration journey by providing access to structured skill development, career preparation, and suitable job opportunities. The platform helps users improve their competencies through learning modules, track their progress, and receive job recommendations based on their skills and interests.

By combining skill-based learning, AI-powered job matching, and career assistance, RESTART aims to create a more inclusive pathway for former inmates to rebuild their confidence, increase their employability, and prepare for sustainable participation in society and the workforce.
